Advance a measured acquisition ray using absolute radial velocity.

Add displacement along the acquisition ray before the rigid/deskew transform. Deskewed inputs subtract the acquisition origin in the header frame before computing the ray. Unknown, implausible, and zero measurements leave XYZ unchanged.
